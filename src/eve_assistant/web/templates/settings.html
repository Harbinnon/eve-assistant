Corporation buyback

If your corporation buys its members' ore at a share of Jita's best buy price, plans compare it with the market. EVE doesn't publish buybacks, so enter it here. Leave both empty to remove it.

Jump bridges

Your alliance's jump bridges (Ansiblex gates), for routes in nullsec. No API lists them, so paste the list your alliance shares: one bridge a line, two system names on it (for example 1DQ1-A » 8QT-H4). Saving replaces the list.

Alerts

Things to do in game go to Discord (a job finished, an order sold or undercut, an empty skill queue, a courier due soon, a stopped extractor, a goal reached), each once, linking back here. Problems with the tool itself go to ntfy. Their addresses are entered at a hidden prompt, not here: run eve-assistant alerts-setup, then eve-assistant alerts-test.

Backups

A copy of your history is saved once a day while the dashboard runs. It can't be downloaded from EVE again, so keep the folder somewhere safe: another drive or a synced folder is best. The README's Backups section says how to move it.
